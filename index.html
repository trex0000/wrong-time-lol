Lot 01 — Case Brief

What happened at the Ashford estate

อ่านเรื่องราวการค้นพบเหตุการณ์ — คลิกคำที่ขีดเส้นทองเพื่อดูคำแปล

Mr. Julian Ashford was a famous diamond dealer. Everyone in the city knew his name and his shop.

On Friday night, Mr. Ashford held a big party at his house — his retirement party. Many old friends came to celebrate.

At 9:00 PM, Mr. Ashford left the party and walked to his private office to check his most famous diamond, the "Ashford Star." It sat inside a locked glass case on his desk.

At 9:45 PM, a waiter walked past the office. The door was open. Mr. Ashford was lying on the floor, not moving. The glass case was open too — and the Ashford Star was gone.

The police doctor arrived quickly. He said Mr. Ashford died sometime between 9:00 PM and 9:30 PM, right in the middle of his own party.

Detective Reyes put down her tea and opened her notebook. Three guests had walked near the office that night. Three different stories. It was time to listen carefully.

Three guests. Three stories. But one small detail doesn't tell time the way Mr. Drake thinks it does, Detective. Look closely — what's wrong with his watch?
Lot 02 — Interview Room

Three guests, three stories

อ่านคำให้การของผู้ต้องสงสัยทั้งสามคนอย่างละเอียด — เวลาและรายละเอียดเล็กๆ สำคัญมาก

01

Mr. Bennett

Business Partner
Wine Cellar · 8:30 PM

Mr. Bennett walked in first, still holding a glass of wine.

"I was in the wine cellar from 8:30 PM, choosing a special bottle for the toast. The cellar is far from the office, so I didn't hear a thing. Yes, Mr. Ashford and I had money problems this year. But I never went near his office tonight."

Detective Reyes wrote one word next to his name: motive.

02

Ms. Lila

Personal Assistant
Balcony · 9:00 PMSaw light off · 9:20 PM

Ms. Lila came in next, her hands still shaking a little.

"I was outside on the balcony at 9:00 PM. I needed some fresh air away from the noise. At about 9:20 PM, I saw the office light turn off. It scared me, so I hurried back inside. I never went near the office door."

Detective Reyes nodded slowly and made a small note about the light.

03

Mr. Drake

Old Friend
Passed office · 9:15 PM

Mr. Drake walked in last, speaking quickly, eager to help.

"I can tell you everything, Detective! At 9:15 PM, I walked past the office door and saw Mr. Ashford talking to another man inside. I checked my digital watch to remember the exact time — the small hand was on the 9, and the big hand was on the 3. Then I heard a loud crash, so I ran away. I was too frightened to go inside."

Detective Reyes looked at Mr. Drake's wrist for a long moment, and said nothing at all.
